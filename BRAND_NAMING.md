# Brand Naming

Status: Gate N — one recommendation awaiting Jan's decision

Updated: 2026-08-29

## Decision requested

Approve **PORT TO KEYS** as the working public service brand, or reject this
naming set. Approval opens visual Gate A only. It does not authorise domain
purchase, trademark filing, website changes, email changes or deployment.

## Brand architecture

```text
PORT TO KEYS
European-side coordination for vehicle imports from China
A service of Casa Buena s.r.o.
```

Czech equivalent:

```text
PORT TO KEYS
Koordinace evropské části dovozu vozidel z Číny
Služba společnosti Casa Buena s.r.o.
```

The brand is the memorable public name. The category line explains the job.
Casa Buena s.r.o. remains visible as the accountable legal operator.

## Naming brief

The name must:

- work as one public brand in Czech and English;
- be short enough to own the researched mirror-hanger artifact;
- point to the verified European-side service boundary;
- feel practical and specific, not luxurious, aspirational or corporate;
- avoid implying a repair shop, carrier, customs authority, freight forwarder
  or guaranteed end-to-end transport;
- remain verbally usable by a Czech client and an international counterparty;
- be distinct from Casa Buena s.r.o. without hiding the legal operator.

The primary conversion remains direct contact. The name is not expected to
explain every service step by itself; the category line and first sentence must
do that without overclaiming.

## Recommendation — PORT TO KEYS

`PORT TO KEYS` compresses the honest service story into two physical endpoints:
the agreed EU port where the standard scope begins, and the keys that represent
vehicle handover. It is understandable without logistics jargon and relatively
easy for a Czech speaker to pronounce (`port tu kíz`).

Why it survives:

- **Scope-native:** `PORT` starts at the exact boundary in the brief rather
  than implying China sourcing or ocean freight.
- **Vehicle-specific:** `KEYS` makes the service tangible without presenting a
  glamorous vehicle or drifting into repair-shop language.
- **Ownable composition:** `PORT → KEYS` gives the physical movement hanger one
  hard, memorable hierarchy instead of another generic webpage layout.
- **Correct register:** it sounds useful and direct rather than luxurious,
  governmental or artificially technical.
- **Bilingual use:** the name can remain unchanged while its factual descriptor
  and scope copy are fully localised.

Recommended first explanatory sentence:

```text
EN  We coordinate the European side from an agreed EU port through to handover.
CZ  Koordinujeme evropskou část od dohodnutého přístavu v EU až po předání.
```

This sentence must remain coordination language. Neither the name nor the copy
may become `door to door`, `we handle everything` or another end-to-end claim.

## Risks and controls

### Descriptive character

The concrete meaning makes the name easy to understand, but may make legal
protection narrower than for an invented word. That tradeoff is acceptable for
the working direction but must be evaluated by a trademark professional before
adoption or filing.

### Implied completion

`KEYS` can sound like a promised final outcome. Every use therefore needs the
category line or nearby scope copy, and the site must keep eligibility,
responsibility, price and timing subject to agreement for each vehicle.

### English name in the Czech market

The words are short and pronounceable, but not self-translating for every Czech
visitor. The Czech category line must appear with the name rather than hiding
behind navigation or legal copy.

## Candidate record

No third finalist is presented merely to make a conventional shortlist. The
screening produced one recommendation and one materially weaker reserve.

| Candidate | Result | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **PORT TO KEYS** | Recommend | Concrete port-to-handover arc; clear, pronounceable and no obvious exact category collision found. |
| `PORT ONWARD` | Reserve only | Relevant and preliminarily clean, but clipped English; `from the port onward` is more idiomatic and too long for the brand. |
| `LANDSIDE` | Reject | Direct logistics-category collisions, including CargoWise Landside and Blackstone Landside. |
| `WAY IN` / `WAYIN` | Reject | Multiple active exact-name businesses; crowded and category-ambiguous. |
| `NAVAZ` / `NAVAŽ` | Reject | Meaning and pronunciation break without Czech diacritics; close transport/automotive names increase confusion. |
| `EU SIDE` / `EUSIDE` | Reject | Active exact `EUSIDE` brand and unnatural English without an article. |
| `ROUTES & RULES` | Reject | Existing logistics-software vocabulary; generic, difficult Czech pronunciation and poor search ownership. |
| `VEHICLE BY VEHICLE` | Reject as name | Descriptive automotive phrase, long and awkward to say in Czech. |
| `FROM PORT` | Reject | Incomplete generic English fragment with no memorable handover endpoint. |

## Preliminary evidence

The checks below are a dated risk screen, not legal clearance.

- An exact-name web search found no obvious active `PORT TO KEYS` business in
  the relevant vehicle-import or European logistics category.
- The official Czech ARES API returned zero Czech economic entities for the
  exact business-name query `Port to Keys` on 2026-08-29.
- Read-only registry queries on 2026-08-29 returned no entry for
  `porttokeys.cz`, `AVAILABLE` for `porttokeys.eu`, and no match for
  `porttokeys.com`. No domain has been reserved; availability can change at any
  time.
- Competing candidates had identifiable category conflicts, including
  [CargoWise Landside](https://www.cargowise.com/landside/),
  [Blackstone Landside](https://blackstonelandside.com/),
  [Wayin](https://www.wayin.io/), [EUSIDE](https://www.euside.com/) and Odoo's
  established [routes and rules](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/18.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/shipping_receiving/daily_operations/use_routes.html)
  terminology.

No reliable formal exact-and-similarity trademark clearance has been completed.
The Czech Industrial Property Office explains that its
[trademark database](https://upv.gov.cz/informacni-zdroje/narodni-databaze/databaze-ochrannych-znamek)
includes Czech marks and relevant WIPO and EU records. Formal review should use
that database together with [TMview](https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/) and
[EUIPO search](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/en/search-ip), include visual,
phonetic and conceptual similarities, and have the relevant service classes
chosen by a trademark professional.
