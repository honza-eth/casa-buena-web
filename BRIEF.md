# Brief

## Objective

Publish a credible, concise company website for Casa buena s.r.o. that supports
business verification and explains its China-origin vehicle import service.

## Audience

- Chinese vehicle exporters who need execution in Europe
- Czech and European counterparties verifying the company

## Truth boundary

Casa Buena coordinates the European side of an import from an agreed European
port. China sourcing, China export formalities, carrier booking and ocean freight
are outside the standard scope. Eligibility, price, timing and responsibility
are confirmed per engagement.

## Success

- Company identity and domain email are visible
- Scope and working model are understandable without overclaiming
- The page works on desktop and mobile
- The production deployment is reliable and easy to update
