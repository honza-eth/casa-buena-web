# Brief

## Objective

Improve the live Casa buena s.r.o. website into a clear, dependable and
radically concise bilingual company site. It must explain Casa Buena's truthful
China-origin vehicle import service immediately and make direct email contact
the obvious next action.

## Audience

- Chinese vehicle exporters who need a European execution coordinator
- European buyers/importers considering a specific vehicle
- Czech and European counterparties verifying Casa Buena

## Languages and URLs

- Complete English version at `/` and canonical `https://casabuena.cz/`
- Complete Czech version at `/cs` and canonical `https://casabuena.cz/cs`
- A clear language switcher and equivalent factual meaning in both languages

## Offer and truth boundary

Casa Buena coordinates the European side of a China-origin vehicle import from
an agreed European port: pre-arrival readiness, carrier release and inland
movement, customs preparation, the technical/registration route and handover.

China sourcing, China export formalities, carrier booking and ocean freight are
outside the standard scope. The European buyer normally remains the importer in
coordination mode. Any transaction in which Casa Buena would buy and resell the
vehicle is assessed separately, case by case.

The site must not imply proven independent end-to-end importing. Do not publish
fixed prices, timelines, country/model coverage, success claims or committed
partner relationships. Eligibility, scope, responsibility, price and timing are
confirmed for each vehicle and engagement.

## Experience requirements

- Retain a recognisable Casa Buena/CB identity within a freely redesigned
  visual system
- Use the minimum copy needed to communicate the complete offer with absolute
  clarity
- Feel calm, competent, practical and intentionally designed rather than
  template-like
- Present a reliable vehicle service, not automotive luxury, aspiration,
  exclusivity or vehicle retail
- Let visible scope, process, responsibility and contact create trust; do not
  use glamorous vehicle imagery or dramatic product-marketing treatment as a
  substitute for service evidence
- Keep direct email as the primary conversion; do not add an enquiry form
- Meet responsive, accessibility and reduced-motion expectations
- Provide correct language metadata, canonical URLs and hreflang alternates

## Success

- Both language versions are complete, equivalent and easy to switch between
- The first viewport answers what Casa coordinates, where its scope begins and
  how to make contact
- No public statement crosses the verified service boundary
- Company identity and the current domain email remain visible
- Desktop and mobile layouts are clear, professional and accessible
- English and Czech rendered-page tests, lint and the production build pass
- The redesign remains local until Jan approves a separate deployment
