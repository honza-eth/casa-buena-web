## A — Cinematic Control

**Thesis:** The vehicle creates immediate desire while Casa Buena appears as a
quiet, exact assurance layer around it.

- **Lineage:** NIO ET9's single immaculate vehicle image, Terminal Industries'
  industrial scale and quiet chrome, Maison Veyrat's material detail.
- **Intended effect:** confident, private, premium and commercially reassuring.
- **Strongest advantage:** the clearest immediate two-order-of-magnitude jump in
  image quality and perceived value.
- **Principal risk:** without disciplined service copy it could be mistaken for
  a vehicle manufacturer, dealer or generic luxury-car landing page.
- **Must not become:** black-and-gold luxury, supercar wallpaper, a configurator
  or a glossy advertisement without a credible operating boundary.
- **Evaluation:** strong Gate A candidate; commercially legible and visually
  polished, but less original than B.

User-facing image: `CASA_BUENA_CONCEPT_A_CINEMATIC_CONTROL.png`

## B — Editorial Dossier

**Thesis:** Casa Buena is an authored European automotive publication and
vehicle file at the same time—cultured enough for the car, exact enough for the
work.

- **Lineage:** Type 7's magazine-cover confidence, Polestar's aggressive
  cropping, Newtwen's grid and annotation precision, the current Casa route
  discipline.
- **Intended effect:** memorable, culturally intelligent, collectible and
  unmistakably designed.
- **Strongest advantage:** the most distinctive direction and the furthest from
  recognisable AI-template aesthetics.
- **Principal risk:** the extreme display type and layered composition require
  careful mobile translation; fake official-document aesthetics must remain
  prohibited.
- **Must not become:** retro scrapbook, Porsche imitation, bureaucratic form,
  fake customs paperwork or ornamental editorial clutter.
- **Evaluation:** Codex recommendation for the requested maximum step change.
  Its first draft was rejected internally for invented shipping data; the
  revised candidate removes those facts and retains only truthful stages.

User-facing image: `CASA_BUENA_CONCEPT_B_EDITORIAL_DOSSIER.png`

## C — European Control Instrument

**Thesis:** The standard boundary and four-stage route become a proprietary
visual instrument whose line resolves into the vehicle itself.

- **Lineage:** Newtwen's technical grid, Terminal Industries' logistics
  restraint, Polestar's object cropping and the existing Casa route graphic.
- **Intended effect:** technically literate, precise, trustworthy and quietly
  futuristic.
- **Strongest advantage:** the service model is visually self-explanatory and
  the route-line/vehicle gesture is ownable.
- **Principal risk:** it is closest to the current dark transit-dossier idea and
  therefore offers the smallest perceived leap; it can also slide into a sci-fi
  dashboard if overdeveloped.
- **Must not become:** HUD, SaaS dashboard, neon cyberpunk, fake technical data
  sheet or a grid used as decoration.
- **Evaluation:** strong clarity and continuity, but not the preferred choice if
  maximal visual reinvention is the primary objective.

User-facing image: `CASA_BUENA_CONCEPT_C_CONTROL_INSTRUMENT.png`

## Comparison

- Choose **A** when immediate premium desire and broad commercial legibility are
  more important than maximum originality.
- Choose **B** when the priority is the strongest graphic authorship and the
  largest break from typical agent-generated web design.
- Choose **C** when operational clarity and continuity with the existing Casa
  identity are more important than a radical aesthetic break.
