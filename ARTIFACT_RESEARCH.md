# Artifact Research

Status: strongest artifact identified; visual use waits for Gate N

Updated: 2026-08-29

## Recommendation — private vehicle movement hanger

Anchor the next visual exploration in a **private, brand-owned hanger for a
vehicle's rear-view mirror**. It is not a shipping label, customs document,
tracking record or claim that the operator physically labels cargo.

This is rooted in real finished-vehicle handling. Wallenius Wilhelmsen's current
cargo-handling instructions require new and used cargo units to be labelled,
make the label readable from 1.5 metres and use the port of discharge in five-
character UN/LOCODE notation plus a VIN or unique identifier. The same manual
explicitly recognises port stickers and mirror hangers as supplementary ways to
identify the destination port.

Sources:

- [Labelling — Cargo Handling Instructions](https://cargohandlinginstruction.walleniuswilhelmsen.com/terminal/labelling-at-the-terminal)
- [Port stickers / mirror hangers](https://cargohandlinginstruction.walleniuswilhelmsen.com/terminal/labelling-at-the-terminal/port-stickers-mirror-hangers)
- [Cargo-label specification](https://cargohandlinginstruction.walleniuswilhelmsen.com/terminal/labelling-at-the-terminal/cargo-label-specification)

## Physical principles to borrow

- a tall, die-cut object with one unmistakable hanging opening;
- one large human-readable destination or handover message;
- a hard hierarchy between the primary message and small operational detail;
- stark print, one functional colour and inexpensive physical material;
- overlaid or replaceable layers that express a change of responsibility;
- a lower tear-off or detachable-looking contact band;
- asymmetrical placement as an object entering the viewport, not a centred
  hero followed by cards.

## Casa-specific translation

The object becomes a **private coordination tag** whose main statement is the
truthful service boundary: from an agreed EU port onward to the European
handover route. It may carry the selected public brand, direct contact and a
plain description of the scope.

The concept should state in small print that it is a private coordination tag,
not a carrier or customs document. It must never pretend that Casa Buena issues
the real port label described by the source.

## Prohibited imitation

- no carrier name, logo or copied label layout;
- no official seal, customs mark or government styling;
- no real or invented VIN, shipment number, customs status or tracking event;
- no functional-looking fake barcode or QR code;
- no specific port code unless it belongs to a clearly identified real case or
  is labelled as a non-operational example;
- no implication that the service includes ocean freight, port operation or
  physical cargo labelling.

## Rejected alternative — inspection/damage form

The European Car-Transport Group's vehicle exception-report language and car
outline diagrams are visually relevant to handover, but using that form as the
identity could imply that the service conducts formal inspections or issues
damage evidence. That capability is not established in the brief. It therefore
remains a research reference, not the visual anchor.

Source: [ECG vehicle inspection publications](https://www.ecgassociation.eu/publications-and-reports/ecg-inspection/)
