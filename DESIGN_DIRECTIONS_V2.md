## Jan's verdict

All three directions are rejected. Although they removed luxury cues, they
translated reliability into another recognisable AI template: white corporate
canvas, condensed headline, red process line, four numbered steps, catalog
icons and predictable content blocks. The result was clean but anonymous and
visually interchangeable with a generic generated infographic.

None of the page grammar, component patterns or proposed hybrids may be reused
as a starting point. The next visual work must wait for a public brand name and
then be hand-authored around one authentic, service-specific physical artifact.
`imagegen` may not generate the complete interface.

The images are first-viewport concepts, not approved copy, production assets or

## A — Service Sheet

**Thesis:** Casa Buena looks like a precisely run vehicle job: the boundary,
required inputs and four service stages are laid out like a first-rate service
sheet.

- **Lineage:** technical manuals, vehicle job sheets, GOV.UK task hierarchy and
  DriiveMe's diagrammatic vehicle treatment.
- **Intended effect:** organised, competent, specific and low-drama.
- **Strongest advantage:** the practical service is unmistakable; there is no
  luxury or product-advertising ambiguity.
- **Principal risk:** it can feel cold, procedural or like workshop software if
  icons and small explanatory copy become too dense.
- **Must not become:** a fake case file, checklist app, tracking interface or
  bureaucratic document.
- **Evaluation:** valid candidate when technical clarity is the priority.

User-facing image: `CASA_BUENA_RELIABLE_CONCEPT_A_SERVICE_SHEET.png`

## B — European Wayfinding

**Thesis:** A single Casa Buena route line turns the agreed-port boundary, four
stages and direct contact into one immediately understandable journey.

- **Lineage:** SBB journey hierarchy, Swiss Post task focus, European transport
  wayfinding and the existing Casa route boundary.
- **Intended effect:** calm, reliable, direct and easy to navigate.
- **Strongest advantage:** the best balance of service clarity, distinctiveness
  and implementation realism; the route line is a useful ownable gesture rather
  than decoration.
- **Principal risk:** excessive signage language could feel institutional or
  impersonal, so the final system needs warmer body copy and contact treatment.
- **Must not become:** a rail-company imitation, map, tracking status, airport
  signage pastiche or public-authority interface.
- **Evaluation:** Codex recommendation for the corrected brief.

User-facing image: `CASA_BUENA_RELIABLE_CONCEPT_B_EUROPEAN_WAYFINDING.png`

## C — Human Handover

**Thesis:** Reliability comes from a visible responsible person and authentic
evidence of ordinary vehicle/document work.

- **Lineage:** AA/RAC service presence, NHS help hierarchy and human-scale local
  professional services.
- **Intended effect:** reachable, accountable, reassuring and personal.
- **Strongest advantage:** it answers the trust question that a purely graphic
  system cannot: who is actually coordinating the work?
- **Principal risk:** the direction is incomplete without authentic Casa Buena
  photography; stock or generated people would falsely imply evidence and
  destroy trust.
- **Must not become:** testimonial marketing, lifestyle photography, smiling
  corporate stock, AI-generated people or a personal-brand site.
- **Evaluation:** valid only if Jan chooses to commission/supply authentic
  service photographs. The concept deliberately shows a photo placeholder.

User-facing image: `CASA_BUENA_RELIABLE_CONCEPT_C_HUMAN_HANDOVER.png`
